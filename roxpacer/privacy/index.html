Privacy Policy

RoxPacer / Race Pacer for HYROX · Last updated: 8 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RoxPacer ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the RoxPacer iOS and Apple Watch app. RoxPacer helps athletes create race pacing plans, track race segments on Apple Watch, review results, and manage an optional Pro subscription.

Operator. RoxPacer is operated by Pavel Korkodinov. For privacy questions, contact ikorkod@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Race plans, race results, segment splits, roxzone timing, history, onboarding state, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system for RoxPacer, and we do not upload your race plans or race results to our own server.

2. Apple Health and workout data

The Apple Watch app may request permission to read heart rate during race tracking and to save workouts to Apple Health. Health data is handled through Apple's HealthKit permission system. RoxPacer uses this data only to show heart rate during a race and to support workout tracking features you start. Health data is not sent to us, sold, used for advertising, or used for tracking.

3. Location data on Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app may use location while you are actively tracking a race or simulation to help detect running segments. Location data is used on device for live race assistance. We do not collect, store, or receive your precise location.

4. Product analytics

RoxPacer collects limited product analytics to understand whether core features work and where the app needs improvement. Analytics are sent to our Supabase project and are not linked to your identity.

Analytics may include:

  • a random per-install identifier and a random per-launch session identifier;
  • app version, build number, iOS version, locale, region, and Pro/free state;
  • event names such as app open, onboarding completed, plan created, analyzer used, race completed, paywall shown, purchase completed, review prompt shown, and share sheet opened;
  • small feature properties, for example division, sex, target minutes, total race minutes, roxzone share percentage, or purchased product identifier.

Analytics do not include your name, email address, Apple ID, IDFA, contacts, payment card details, precise location, HealthKit heart-rate samples, or full race history. We do not use analytics for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

RoxPacer Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment details and subscription management. RoxPacer receives subscription entitlement status from Apple so the app can unlock Pro features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

6. Third-party service providers

We use Apple services for App Store distribution, StoreKit subscriptions, HealthKit permissions, workout handling, and iCloud/device functionality provided by iOS and watchOS. We use Supabase only for append-only product analytics events described above.

7. Data retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, reset app data where available, or uninstall the app. Analytics events may be retained while needed to measure product health and improve the app. Because analytics identifiers are random and not tied to an account, we may not be able to identify a specific user's analytics rows without information from your device.

8. Your choices

You can deny Health and Location permissions in iOS or watchOS Settings. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. You can delete local app data by deleting the app from your device.

9. Children

RoxPacer is not directed to children under the age where parental consent is required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as RoxPacer changes. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact ikorkod@gmail.com.

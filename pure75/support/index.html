Pure 75 Support

For 75 Hard Tracker: Pure 75, contact developer Pavel Korkodinov at ikorkod@gmail.com.

Get help

Include the app version, iOS version, a short description of the issue, and the steps that led to it. Do not send your Apple password, authentication tokens, full payment-card details, or private progress photos.

For an existing purchase, try Settings → Restore Purchases using the Apple account that made the purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests; see Apple's refund instructions.

Privacy and data requests

Version 1.0.6 is the currently published version. In 1.0.6 and later, use Settings → Delete Buddy Account to delete the online Buddy account identified for that installation. Your local challenge history, photos, and purchases remain. The operation requires a successful server response. If the app reports that an older account cannot safely be identified, contact support for help; it will not silently delete or replace that account. Version 1.0.5 and earlier do not include this control.

Send privacy, access, correction, or deletion requests to the same email address with “Pure 75 privacy” in the subject. Please identify which app and which feature your request concerns. We may need additional information to locate the relevant randomly generated installation or Buddy identifier. An email address is not automatically linked to those identifiers. Do not send health records or photos merely to identify yourself.

Unpairing Buddy removes the active pair and its day-state records; it does not delete the underlying account or separate analytics records. Deleting the app does not automatically delete server records. See the Privacy Policy for the current behavior and limitations.

Apple Watch — upcoming version 1.0.7

Version 1.0.7 is upcoming and has not yet been released. Its Watch app and complication will show a read-only summary from your paired iPhone: challenge day and mode, completed rules, and water progress. Log and edit progress in Pure 75 on iPhone.

After installing 1.0.7 on both devices, open Pure 75 on iPhone and then on Watch. Use Refresh from iPhone on Watch when the devices are connected. If the Watch says the iPhone is disconnected or asks for today's progress, open the iPhone app and allow the paired devices to reconnect. Check the displayed update time; an older summary is not a new check-in. The feature does not upload your challenge database or photos to CloudKit.
